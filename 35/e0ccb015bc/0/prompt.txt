I've read through the produce form, checkout, fees, payments, stock, the consumer feed and the Android app. I haven't changed anything. Here is the full analysis.

Wholesale + Points + Subscription: pre-build analysis

0. What the requirement means

The flow: Farmer lists produce for retail, wholesale or both → Aggregator buys wholesale (fixed packs, or above a minimum quantity) → aggregator resells to households.

The money side:
- Aggregators pay a monthly subscription plus a fee on every order.
- Aggregators earn points on purchases and spend them on new purchases.
- Farmers earn points on sales and see their share % of marketplace business.

Today none of this exists:
- No wholesale mode or minimum quantity.
- No points or wallet.
- No subscriptions.

---

1. Missing details and edge cases

Wholesale listing
1. Wholesale price: is it per kg or per pack? If the produce is sold as "Both", does it have two prices (retail and wholesale)?
2. "Both" stock: is there one stock pool or two? If one, a single 400 kg wholesale order can empty retail stock in one go.
3. Pack details: pack type (bag, crate, box) and pack weight. Does the pack size apply only to kg, or also to pieces and litres?
4. "Multiple of packages or multiple of package sizes": can a farmer offer more than one pack size (10 kg and 25 kg bags)? Mixed pack sizes make the cart and stock logic much harder.
5. No pack, minimum quantity only: after the minimum (say 100 kg), can the buyer order any amount (101.5 kg?), or only in steps of 1, 5 or 10 kg? Is there a maximum per order?
6. Stock below the minimum: if only 60 kg is left and the minimum is 100 kg, is the listing hidden, or can the farmer sell the last lot?
7. Harvest model: stock lives per harvest today. Is wholesale stock also per harvest? Can an aggregator pre-book an upcoming harvest?

Transport ("can you transport anywhere in AP?")
8. If Yes: who pays, and how is it priced (per km, per kg, a fixed amount, or included in the price)? Is it really any district, or does the farmer choose districts?
9. If No: the aggregator collects from the farm. Which address: the farm location or the existing pickup points?
10. Delivery time promise, e.g. "within 2 days of approval".

Orders
11. Weight difference: if 100 kg was ordered and 97 kg was delivered, is the difference refunded? What tolerance is allowed?
12. Partial supply: if the farmer has only 300 of 400 kg, does the farmer decline the whole order, or deliver part of it?
13. Quality complaint at delivery: who decides? The moderator? What is the evidence (photos, weight slip)?
14. Cancellation: can the aggregator cancel after the farmer has packed or dispatched?

Payment
15. Is the wholesale order paid fully online in advance, as advance + balance, or as cash on delivery? A ₹50,000 order may hit UPI per-transaction limits (often ₹1 lakh).
16. Payout to farmers is manual today (src/lib/payout.ts). Wholesale amounts are large, so manual transfers carry more risk of mistakes.

Points
17. Farmer points: what can they be used for? This is not stated. Points with no use will be ignored.
18. Farmer "share %": share of what? All sales or only wholesale? This month or all time?
19. Aggregator points: earning rate (1% of purchase?), point value (1 point = ₹1?), the maximum share of an order that can be paid with points, and the expiry period.
20. On cancellation or refund: are earned points taken back? Are redeemed points returned?
21. Who pays for redeemed points? If an aggregator pays ₹100 in points, the farmer must still receive the full price. GoGrameen must cover it.

Subscription
22. Price, billing cycle, a free trial, and GST on top.
23. What exactly is locked without a subscription? Only wholesale buying, or also retail selling?
24. Existing aggregators: GodhanTadepalligudem made 91 of the 149 paid orders in the PDF. Do they get grandfathered or a free period?
25. Renewal: automatic (UPI AutoPay) or a manual monthly payment link? How many grace days after expiry?

3. Requirements by area

UI / UX
- Produce form (farmer, aggregator and moderator): a Retail / Wholesale / Both choice. Wholesale shows: pack yes/no, pack weight and type, or minimum quantity, the wholesale price, and transport yes/no with its details. The form already has 30+ fields, so the wholesale part should be a collapsible section.
- New "Wholesale market" page for aggregators: filters for district, produce, "farmer transports" and pack size. Cards show the pack price and the per-kg price.
- Wholesale cart: a pack counter ("3 bags × 25 kg = 75 kg"), and a clear message when the quantity is below the minimum.
- Farmer orders page: a Wholesale tag on orders and a wholesale/retail filter.
- Points screen: balance, history and level for farmers; balance and a "use points" toggle at checkout for aggregators.
- Subscription screen: plan, expiry date, Pay/Renew button, and an expiry banner.
- Moderator: the same wholesale fields on ListingForm, a subscription list, points adjustment, and a fee setting.

Backend / API
- Order placement: a wholesale branch in src/app/api/orders/place/route.ts that checks the channel, pack multiples or minimum, buyer type, subscription, points and transport. This must be checked on the server, not only in the UI.
- New routes:
  - Wholesale listings feed (aggregators only)
  - Subscription: create, verify, webhook
  - Points: balance, history, redeem
  - Moderator screens for all three
- A daily cron: expire points and mark subscriptions as expired.

Database (new; nothing exists today)
- produce_listings: sale_channel, wholesale_price, pack_size, pack_label, wholesale_min_qty, wholesale_step, farmer_transports, transport_districts, transport_charge
- orders: channel, pack_count, points_redeemed, points_earned
- New tables: points_ledger, aggregator_subscriptions, subscription_plans, plus new fields on platform_settings

Permissions
- Only a buyer whose phone links to an aggregator account can buy wholesale. The check goes through src/lib/sellerBuyerLink.ts, which already links buyer and seller accounts by phone.
- Guest checkout is not allowed for wholesale.
- The points and subscription tables must be writable only by the server (service role), like payout_accounts.

Validation
- Pack multiples or minimum quantity
- Stock at least the ordered quantity
- Wholesale price greater than 0
- Pack size within sensible limits
- Points used no more than the balance and the cap
- Subscription active

Notifications (WhatsApp)
New templates are needed:
- Wholesale order to the farmer
- Order confirmation to the aggregator
- Dispatch
- Subscription reminder
- Subscription expired
- Points earned

Every new template needs Meta approval, which takes days, so submit them early.

Reports
- Farmer report split into retail vs wholesale
- Moderator order report
- Points statement
- Subscription revenue
- Platform income (fees + subscriptions)
- A wholesale invoice for aggregators

Mobile
- The pack selector and the wholesale cards must fit at 390 px width.
- Every label in Telugu + English.
- Keep wholesale pages light for 4G connections.

5. Improvements and hidden expectations

The client will probably expect these even though they didn't mention them:
1. Show the per-kg price next to the pack price, e.g. ₹1,000/bag (₹40/kg).
2. Lot photos and a weight-slip photo at dispatch, as evidence in disputes.
3. A weight tolerance rule (±2%) with automatic refund of any shortfall.
4. An "Accept partial" option for farmers.
5. "Resell this" for aggregators: after a wholesale purchase, one tap creates a retail listing with the grower already linked. This connects wholesale to retail and to the transparency goal.
6. New-listing alerts: when a farmer lists wholesale, subscribed aggregators in nearby districts get a WhatsApp message.
7. Levels for farmers instead of a raw share %, e.g. "Silver farmer, 2,300 points to Gold". Levels unlock a lower fee, priority in listings, and later the transport/packing services. This gives the points a real use.
8. Subscription that saves money: 2–3 months free, and the plan lowers the per-order fee.
9. A wholesale invoice (PDF): aggregators need it for their accounts.
10. Price guideline per crop for wholesale: moderator price guidelines already exist for retail.

Phase 3: fees + subscription (needs Phase 2) — M/L

Phase 4: points (needs Phases 2 and 3) — L

Phase 5: future
- Packing and transport services for privileged farmers

Every phase ships with tests in the same commit, gets the same changes on the Moderator side, and goes to staging first.

7. Risks, assumptions and blockers

Blockers
- The client's answers, especially who pays the fee, the points rules and the subscription locks.
- RLS still open on prod for produce_listings, orders and harvests.
- 59 uncommitted files on staging.
- WhatsApp template approval time.

Risks
- Money liability: points are a promise to pay. Clear expiry and caps are needed.
- Points abuse through dual accounts or self-buying.
- Large payments: UPI limits and refund size; the manual payout process.
- Subscription pushback: aggregators may stop listing, and one aggregator drives most of today's orders.
- Staging is far ahead of prod: many features are still unmerged, so adding more widens the gap.

Legal and tax (check with a CA or lawyer)
- AP market rules: wholesale trade in notified produce outside a market yard may need a trader licence or market fee.
- GST: 18% on the subscription and fees. Possible TCS on taxable items (ghee, oil, soap are sold today).
- FSSAI registration for aggregators.

Assumptions I made
- One pack size per listing in version 1.
- Wholesale needs its own cart.
- The platform pays for redeemed points.
- The Android app stays retail-only for now.
