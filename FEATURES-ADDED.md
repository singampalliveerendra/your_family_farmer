# ✨ New Features Added — YourFamilyFarmer

A simple summary of the 10 improvements we added to make payments, orders, and
refunds reliable and trustworthy. No technical words — just what each one does
for your customers and farmers.

---

## 1. 🧾 Order ID for every order
Every order now gets a clear ID like **`YFF-20260523-0001`**.
It shows on the customer's order page, the farmer's dashboard, and the receipt.
**Why it helps:** easy to talk about a specific order with support or the farmer
— "my order YFF-…" — instead of guessing.

## 2. 💸 Automatic refunds
If a farmer declines an order the customer already paid for, the money is
**refunded automatically** — no one has to do it by hand.
**Why it helps:** no angry customers waiting for their money; no manual mistakes.

## 3. 🔁 No double charges or double orders
If the internet is slow and a customer taps "Pay" or "Place order" twice, the
app is smart enough to **not** create two orders or charge them twice.
**Why it helps:** protects customers from being charged extra on weak networks.

## 4. 🛡️ Reliable payment confirmation (webhook)
Even if a customer's phone dies or loses signal **right after paying**, the
payment still gets confirmed automatically, because the payment company tells
our system directly.
**Why it helps:** stops the worst complaint — "money gone but order not placed."
*(Needs a one-time setup in the client's Razorpay account.)*

## 5. 🧾 Printable payment receipt
After paying, the customer can tap **"View receipt"** to see and print/save a
clean receipt with the order ID, amount, farmer name, and date.
**Why it helps:** customers feel secure having proof of payment.

## 6. ⏱️ Cancel within 30 minutes
A customer can **cancel their own order within 30 minutes**, as long as the
farmer hasn't confirmed it yet. If they paid, they're refunded automatically.
**Why it helps:** gives customers a fair, stress-free way to undo a mistake.

## 7. 📊 Refund status tracking
When a refund happens, the customer sees its progress step by step:
**Refund initiated → Processing → Credited.**
**Why it helps:** removes the "where is my money?" anxiety.

## 8. 🚦 Order status timeline
Customers can follow their order's journey — **Placed → Confirmed → Ready for
pickup**, or for home delivery, all the way to **Delivered**.
**Why it helps:** customers always know what's happening, fewer "where's my
order?" calls.

## 9. 🔔 Live updates for farmers
The farmer's dashboard shows **new orders instantly**, and refreshes the moment
they reopen the app — even on a weak connection.
**Why it helps:** farmers never miss an order.

## 10. 📄 Buyer protection page
A simple page explaining how payments are safe, how refunds work, and how to get
help — linked from the orders page.
**Why it helps:** builds trust, especially for first-time online buyers.

---

## In one line
These features make sure **money is never lost, orders never lie, and customers
always know what's happening** — the same things that make big apps like Amazon
and Swiggy feel trustworthy.
