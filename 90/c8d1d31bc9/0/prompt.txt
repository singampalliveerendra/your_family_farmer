1. **B2B (Bulk Selling):** Aggregators and sellers can sell produce in bulk by setting a minimum order quantity. Buyers must meet the seller's minimum quantity requirements.

2. **B2C (Retail Selling):** Farmers who purchase produce from B2B sellers can resell that produce on our platform to regular consumers, such as households, in any quantity suitable for retail purchases.

### Our Main Goal
Our primary goal is to help consumers understand where their food comes from, who produced it, and how it reaches them. We also display farmers' contact numbers to build trust and transparency.

### The Main Business Challenge
Customers may initially purchase produce in bulk through GoGrameen, but after obtaining the seller's contact number, they may contact the seller directly for future purchases. This could reduce repeat orders and platform revenue.
