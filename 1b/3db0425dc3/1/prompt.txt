This document explores ways to calculate delivery charge for the consumer receiving harvests from a single farmer or multiple farmers as part of the same checkout process. 
There could be multiple harvests from a single farmer as well. 

Case1: 1 Harvest and 1 Farmer. A fixed charge of Rs. 30/- is added to the cost of the order. 

Case2: 2 Harvests from the same Farmer. Again a fixed charge of Rs.30/- is added to the cost of the order. 

Case3: 1 Harvest from 1 Farmer and Harvest from 2nd Farmer in the same checkout. Since the delivery boy will collect the harvests from 2 farmers an additional Rs.15/- will be added to the overall cost and it turns out to be Rs.45/-. The same for each additional farmer a Rs.15/- will be added. 

Now the real complexity comes what if the farmer rejects the order or the consumer cancels the order. 

Farmer Rejection/User Cancellation 
Case 4: If there is only 1 item and the farmer rejects the item, then we must refund the entire delivery charge of Rs30/-

Case 5: If there are multiple items ordered at once, and the farmer rejects one of the items, then we do not need to refund the delivery charge.

Case 6: If the order contains 2 farmers and 1 item each for them. Then if one farmer rejects then the extra Rs.15/- collected should be refunded.

Rs. 30/- and Rs.15/- can be variable. So, give the moderator provision to update these charges. First one is Delivery Base Charge, second one is additional delivery charge.

For customer and consumer, the delivery charge should be visible clearly.
